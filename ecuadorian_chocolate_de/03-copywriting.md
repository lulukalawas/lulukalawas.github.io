# Brand & Landing Page Copywriting: Ecuadorian Arriba Nacional Chocolate

- **Headline**: Single-Origin Ecuadorian Chocolate. Grown for Character.
- **Subheadline**: Crafted from heritage Arriba Nacional cacao—bringing bright floral aromatics and deep cocoa structure to curious palates across Germany.

## Value Proposition Blocks
1. **Heritage Arriba Nacional Cacao**
   Distinct floral, fruit, and cocoa notes rooted in Ecuador's fine-flavor tradition rather than generic mass-market blends.
2. **Honest Origin & Care**
   Clear sourcing transparency and temperature-aware logistics designed for reliable arrival across German climates.
3. **Built for Tasting & Gifting**
   Refined presentation suited to specialty cafés, Feinkost shelves, personal tasting, and thoughtful gifting.
